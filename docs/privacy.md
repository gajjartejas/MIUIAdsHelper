# Privacy Policy

Public webpage: https://gajjartejas.github.io/miui-ads-helper-website/privacy/

MIUI Ads Helper is an independent app provided by Tejas Gajjar. This policy explains data use in the current app and on this public information website.

Effective date: 9 November 2026

## Information the app processes

- **Local compatibility checks:** the app checks packages associated with its settings guides to show which guides apply. The updated app keeps these results on the device; it does not report installed or missing guide inventories, package availability, counts, or category summaries to analytics.
- **Standard analytics:** Firebase Analytics processes screen views, purchase-flow events, and its standard SDK data, which may include app-instance identifiers, device/software information, and usage metrics under Google’s policies. The updated app does not obtain a device’s unique ID to set an analytics user ID or attach custom device-identifying properties.
- **Crash and diagnostic data:** Firebase Crashlytics receives errors and technical diagnostics under its own data-processing policies.
- **Local preferences:** appearance, language, view mode, and purchase status are stored on the device.

## Why this information is used

Local compatibility results help filter guides without transmitting an app inventory. Standard analytics helps understand app use, and diagnostics helps investigate failures. Crash reporting is enabled automatically in the current configuration. The app does not currently provide an in-app analytics opt-out switch.

## Changes from older app versions

Earlier development versions sent a device identifier, guide-associated package availability, and related summaries to Firebase Analytics. That custom reporting has been removed in the updated source. Before sending new custom analytics events, the updated app clears the legacy custom user ID and related user properties. This does not delete historical data already held by Google or change older app versions that have not been updated.

## Optional purchases

Where offered, purchases and purchase restoration are handled through Google Play Billing. The app processes purchase results to enable purchased features and stores purchase status locally. Purchase failures are recorded as non-fatal Firebase Crashlytics diagnostics with the billing stage, standardized SDK error code, Google Play response/subresponse codes when available, and a recognized in-app product ID. This diagnostic reporter does not upload raw native error messages, receipts, purchase tokens, order IDs, payment-card details, or custom device identifiers. User cancellations and pending payments are treated as normal outcomes, not diagnostic errors. Payment details are handled by Google Play rather than entered into this website.

## Support messages

If you contact Tejas Gajjar, information you choose to send, such as an email address, device details, or an issue description, is used to respond and investigate. GitHub issues are public; avoid sharing private information there.

## This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, cookie banners, or account sign-in. It does not set application cookies. If you use the light/dark mode button, it stores only your theme choice in browser local storage on your device. This preference is not sent to the developer. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data. Without a saved choice, the site follows your device’s color-scheme preference. GitHub may process access logs, including IP addresses, as described in the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement). External links are governed by the destination’s policy.

## Retention and your choices

Local app preferences remain until cleared through Android app storage controls or removed with the app, subject to your device’s backup behavior. Data held by third-party providers follows the applicable service settings and policies. Clearing local app data does not automatically delete previously transmitted analytics or diagnostics.

Contact [gajjartejas26@gmail.com](mailto:gajjartejas26@gmail.com) with privacy, access, or deletion requests. Include only the information needed to identify your request. Some data may not be identifiable or retrievable by the developer.
