# Frequently Asked Questions

Public webpage: https://gajjartejas.github.io/miui-ads-helper-website/faq/

## Which phones are supported?

The guides focus on Xiaomi’s MIUI apps and system settings. Availability varies by region, MIUI or HyperOS release, and app version. Not every setting is available on every device.

## Which Android version do I need?

The current app source targets Android 7.0 (API 24) and later. Check the Google Play listing for the requirements of the version available to your device; older releases may differ.

## Does it block all ads?

No. MIUI Ads Helper is a guide to built-in ad and recommendation settings, not a network-wide ad blocker. It does not remove ads from every app or website.

## Do I need root?

No. The guides use settings available on the device. The app cannot grant access to restricted manufacturer screens.

## Why does Open show App info?

If none of the known settings screens can open, App info may be used as a fallback. Follow the manual steps in the guide to locate the specific recommendation option.

## Why is a guide marked as not installed?

The associated package was not detected. Regional packages and device software can vary. You can still read the guide under All guides.

## Can I undo a change?

Yes. Open the same settings and change the relevant toggle again. The app itself does not automatically alter your preferences.

## Does the app collect data?

The current app uses Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. The updated app no longer reports guide-associated package availability or sets an analytics user ID from a device identifier. Standard Firebase SDK analytics and crash reporting remain. Read the [Privacy Policy](https://gajjartejas.github.io/miui-ads-helper-website/privacy/) for details.

## How can I contribute?

See the [translation page](https://gajjartejas.github.io/miui-ads-helper-website/translate/), report a problem, or explore the [source repository](https://github.com/gajjartejas/MIUIAdsHelper).
